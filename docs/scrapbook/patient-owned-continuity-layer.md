# Patient-owned continuity layer

## Idea

Loom's distinctive role in healthcare may be neither medical provider nor electronic patient record, but a **patient-owned continuity layer** across providers, institutions, and time.

The continuity of an investigation should not disappear when a clinician changes, a treatment episode ends, or an institution exports only a final report.

## Working-model example

A patient-controlled longitudinal document or structure might preserve:

- observations and recurring patterns
- current hypotheses
- counterevidence and contradictory evidence
- open questions
- interventions or experiments tried
- outcomes
- clinician hypotheses with provenance
- patient responses or disputes
- next observations that would discriminate between hypotheses

The point is not to force medicine into software-development metaphors. It is to preserve the accumulated state of an investigation instead of repeatedly reconstructing it from isolated appointments and summary letters.

## Agent role

An authorized agent could use this continuity layer to identify unresolved questions, contradictions between new and older records, changes since the previous appointment, and material relevant to a forthcoming encounter.

The agent should not silently promote its synthesis to canonical truth. Its outputs remain attributable derived material.
